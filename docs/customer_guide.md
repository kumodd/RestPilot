# Customer Guide

Welcome to the RestPilot dining experience. This guide outlines how customers use the web-based ordering system. **No app installation is required.**

## 1. Accessing the Digital Menu
* Use your smartphone camera or a QR scanner app to scan the QR code located on your table.
* You will be taken directly to the restaurant's digital menu in your mobile browser.
* The system automatically identifies your specific table.

## 2. Browsing and Ordering
* **Browse**: Navigate through menu categories, view high-quality images, read descriptions, and check prices.
* **Customize**: Select options, variants (e.g., size, spice level), and add-ons for your chosen items.
* **Special Instructions**: Add notes for the kitchen if needed.
* **Add to Cart**: Add items to your cart as you decide.

## 3. Placing the Order
* Review your cart.
* Provide your name (optional) and phone number (if required by the restaurant).
  * *Note: Your browser can securely save this information to auto-fill on future visits to this restaurant.*
* Tap **Place Order**.

## 4. Order Verification
* After placing your order, a waiter will briefly visit your table.
* They will verify the order with you to ensure perfection and may suggest some popular or complementary items you might enjoy.
* Once the waiter confirms, your order is instantly sent to the kitchen.

## 5. Live Order Tracking
* Keep the browser window open to track your order in real-time.
* You will see the status update automatically: 
  `Order Received` -> `Waiter Verified` -> `Kitchen Preparing` -> `Ready` -> `Served`

## 6. Modifying Your Order
* **Adding Items**: You can scan the QR code again or tap "Add More Items" on your tracking page to order additional food or drinks.
* **Removing/Changing Items**: If you need to cancel or change an item that has already been confirmed, please ask your waiter for assistance.
