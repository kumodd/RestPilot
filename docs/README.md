# RestPilot - User Manuals

Welcome to the RestPilot documentation. RestPilot is a Multi-Tenant QR Restaurant Ordering & Waiter Service Platform that combines the convenience of QR-based digital menus with human waiter interaction.

## Available Manuals

* [Restaurant Owner & Manager Manual](./restaurant_owner_manual.md) - Guide for managing restaurants, branches, tables, menus, staff, and viewing reports.
* [Waiter Manual](./waiter_manual.md) - Guide for the Waiter mobile application (order verification, recommendations, and service).
* [Kitchen Manual](./kitchen_manual.md) - Guide for the Kitchen tablet application (order preparation and status updates).
* [Customer Guide](./customer_guide.md) - Overview of the customer web experience (scanning QR, ordering, and tracking).
* [Platform Admin Manual](./platform_admin_manual.md) - Guide for platform administrators managing tenants and subscriptions.

## Core Operational Loop

The RestPilot platform is built around a unified operational loop designed to enhance, rather than replace, human service:

1. **Scan**: Customer scans table-specific QR code.
2. **Browse & Order**: Customer browses digital menu and places an order.
3. **Waiter Verification**: Waiter receives notification and physically verifies the order at the table.
4. **Recommendation**: Waiter suggests owner-approved items (popular, new, add-ons).
5. **Kitchen Preparation**: Verified order is sent to the kitchen in real-time.
6. **Service**: Waiter serves the prepared order.
7. **Tracking**: Customer tracks order status in real-time.

For detailed instructions on each role, please refer to the specific manuals linked above.
