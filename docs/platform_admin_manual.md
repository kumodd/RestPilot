# Platform Admin Manual

This manual is for the top-level Platform Administrators who manage the SaaS RestPilot system.

## 1. Dashboard Overview
* Log in to the `/admin` portal.
* View platform-wide metrics including Total Active Restaurants, Total Owners, Total Monthly Orders, and Gross Merchandise Value (GMV).
* Monitor overall system health and database performance.

## 2. Managing Tenants (Restaurant Owners)
* **View Owners**: Browse the list of registered restaurant owners.
* **Manage Subscriptions**: View and manage billing plans (Free/Trial, Standard, Premium) for each owner account.
* **Account Status**: Suspend or reactivate owner accounts in case of billing issues or policy violations.

## 3. Platform Settings
* Configure global settings, platform branding, and default limits for different subscription tiers.
* Manage global feature flags for rolling out new capabilities to tenants.

## 4. Support and Analytics
* **Impersonation**: Securely access a restaurant owner's dashboard (with audit logging) to provide technical support.
* **Platform Analytics**: Generate reports on platform growth, tenant retention, and aggregate order volume to drive business decisions.
