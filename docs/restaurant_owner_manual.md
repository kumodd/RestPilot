# Restaurant Owner & Manager Manual

This manual provides instructions for Restaurant Owners and Managers to configure and operate their restaurants on the RestPilot platform.

## 1. Dashboard Overview
Upon logging in to the web dashboard, you will see a live overview of your restaurant's operations:
* **Live Orders Board**: Monitor orders as they move through different states (Awaiting Verification -> Confirmed -> Preparing -> Ready -> Served).
* **Metrics Snapshot**: View today's total orders, sales, active orders, and average order value.
* **Restaurant Switcher**: If you own multiple restaurants, use the top navigation dropdown to switch between them.

## 2. Managing Restaurants and Branches
* **Restaurant Profile**: Update your restaurant's branding, including name, logo, cover image, address, phone number, and operating hours.
* **Branches**: Create and manage multiple physical locations under a single restaurant brand.

## 3. Table & QR Management
Each physical table requires a unique QR code for customers to scan.
* **Create Tables**: Navigate to **Tables**, add a new table, and assign it to a branch.
* **Generate QR Codes**: Select a table to generate its unique QR code. 
* **Download & Print**: Download the generated QR codes to print and place on the physical tables.
* **Table Status**: View real-time status of each table (Available, Ordering, Occupied, Ready to Serve, Bill Requested).

## 4. Menu Management
The digital menu is what customers see when they scan a QR code.
* **Categories**: Create categories (e.g., Starters, Mains, Beverages) and arrange their display order.
* **Items**: Add menu items with name, description, price, and image.
* **Modifiers & Add-ons**: Configure item variants (e.g., Size: Regular/Large) and add-ons (e.g., Extra Cheese).
* **Badges**: Mark items as **Popular**, **New**, **Special**, or **Recommended** to highlight them to customers and waiters.
* **Availability**: Instantly mark items as out-of-stock or temporarily unavailable.

## 5. Waiter Suggestions Engine
Configure the intelligent recommendations your waiters will offer customers during order verification.
* **Create Suggestions**: Go to **Suggestions** and create rules. For example, prompt waiters to suggest a specific dessert when a customer orders a main course.
* **Set Priorities**: Prioritize high-margin or promotional items for waiters to recommend.

## 6. Staff Management
Create accounts and manage access for your staff.
* **Roles**: Assign roles such as Manager, Waiter, or Chef.
* **Branch Assignment**: Assign staff to specific branches so they only see relevant orders.

## 7. Reports & Analytics
Access detailed insights into your restaurant's performance.
* **Sales Reports**: View daily sales, total orders, and average order values.
* **Item Performance**: Track top-selling items and categories.
* **Waiter Analytics**: Monitor waiter performance, specifically tracking the acceptance rate and revenue generated from their recommendations.
