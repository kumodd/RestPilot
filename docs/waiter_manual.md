# Waiter Manual

This manual provides instructions for Waiters using the RestPilot Flutter mobile application. The waiter plays a crucial role in verifying orders and providing human service and recommendations.

## 1. Getting Started
* Download and install the Waiter App on your mobile device.
* Log in using your credentials provided by the restaurant manager.
* Ensure you are assigned to the correct branch if your restaurant has multiple locations.

## 2. Receiving New Orders
* When a customer places an order via the QR code, you will receive a real-time push notification and sound alert.
* Open the app to view the **New Orders** queue.
* Tap on an order to see the table number, items ordered, total amount, and any special customer instructions.

## 3. Order Verification & Recommendations
This is the most important step in the workflow.
1. **Physical Visit**: Walk to the table indicated on the order.
2. **Verify Details**: Verbally confirm the order with the customer to ensure accuracy.
3. **Make Suggestions**: The app will display a **Suggestions** section based on the customer's order.
   * Ask the customer: "Would you like anything else?"
   * Read the suggested items (e.g., popular dishes, complementary sides, or special promotions).
   * Note any internal instructions (e.g., "Suggest dessert for groups of 3+").
4. **Modify Order**: If the customer accepts a suggestion or wants to add/remove items, use the **Add Item** or **Modify** buttons directly in the app.
5. **Confirm Order**: Once the order is finalized with the customer, tap **Confirm Order**. This instantly sends the order to the kitchen.

## 4. Monitoring Order Status
* **Active Orders**: Track the status of all your confirmed orders.
* **Ready for Service**: You will receive a notification when the kitchen marks an order as "Ready".
* **Serve**: Deliver the food to the table and mark the order as **Served** in the app.

## 5. Billing and Completion
* If the customer requests the bill through their phone, you will receive a notification.
* Process the payment according to your restaurant's procedures.
* Mark the order as **Completed** to free up the table in the system.
